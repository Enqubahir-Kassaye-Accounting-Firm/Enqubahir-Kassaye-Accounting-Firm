## 4. Content

Services:

- Name: Accounting & Bookkeeping
  For whom: Businesses that need their books kept every month, at his office or remotely.
  In 1–2 lines: Sets up the accounting system and keeps the books every month, so the figures are ready when tax or audit time comes.
  What's included: Accounting system and internal controls setup; monthly bookkeeping and ledgers; bank reconciliation; monthly financial reports; office or remote service
- Name: Audit Services
  For whom: Companies that need a statutory audit or must get their records ready for one.
  In 1–2 lines: Audits financial records and internal controls in line with AABE requirements, and clears backlogs before the audit.
  What's included: Statutory audit; pre-audit preparation; clearing backlog years; internal audit and risk assessment; review of records and internal controls
- Name: Tax Consultancy & Filing
  For whom: Businesses that file VAT, withholding tax and employee income tax every month.
  In 1–2 lines: Calculates, declares and files taxes on time, and stands with the client during tax authority assessments.
  What's included: Company income tax calculation and filing; monthly VAT, withholding tax and employee income tax; tax planning; review of past filings; representation during tax assessments
- Name: Financial Reporting (IFRS & IPSAS)
  For whom: Companies and non-profits that must present financial statements to a standard.
  In 1–2 lines: Prepares the balance sheet, income statement and cash flow statement to IFRS or IPSAS.
  What's included: Balance sheet; income statement; cash flow statement; IFRS statements for companies; IPSAS statements where required
- Name: Payroll Management
  For whom: Employers with staff on payroll.
  In 1–2 lines: Runs the monthly payroll and calculates and files employee income tax with it.
  What's included: Monthly payroll processing; employee income tax calculation and filing; payroll records kept with the books
- Name: Business Advisory
  For whom: People starting a business and companies planning a change.
  In 1–2 lines: Helps register and license a business, and advises on investment, feasibility and restructuring.
  What's included: Business registration and licensing; investment advice; financial feasibility studies; company restructuring; accounting software setup
