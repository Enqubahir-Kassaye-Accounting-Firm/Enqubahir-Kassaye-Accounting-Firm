# Packages

This template delivers the **Digital Presence** package from the Service &
Solutions Guide. The other packages (Business Website System, Customer Flow
System, Custom) need an admin panel or software this template does not have.
The price is in the Service Guide and the quote, not here.

## Digital Presence — what is included

- Website built from the client's Brand Guideline and Blueprint: Home, About,
  Services (a page per service, up to 12), Projects (a page per project, if
  any), Privacy, Terms.
- Homepage sections in the agreed order: hero, about, services, projects,
  partners, testimonials, contact, call to action (a section with no entries
  hides itself and its menu link).
- Mobile-first, fast, light and dark theme, SEO metadata, sitemap, structured
  data, generated favicon and share image.
- Contact: call, WhatsApp / Telegram, email, map, chat button, contact form
  (Web3Forms, in the client's name), booking link.
- First-draft website text from the Blueprint, corrected by the client.
- Brand Guideline (a gift at handoff).
- Domain registration, hosting and deployment.
- **First year included:** domain, hosting, maintenance, support, and small
  content updates (text, photos, contact details). From the second year, a
  yearly fee agreed with the client before renewal.

## Payment

50% deposit to start (SOP step 0); 50% before going live (SOP step 7). An
exception is the owner's call and is recorded in the project sheet.

## Revisions and approvals

- **2 revision rounds.** A round is one list of changes from the client, sent
  in one message or document, applied together. A message after a list has
  been applied starts the next round. Included in a round: wording, images,
  contact details, section order or on/off, colours, adding or removing
  services, projects, testimonials and partners within the limits above.
- **Approved:** the client writes it in the group after the last round (SOP
  step 6). It covers the Privacy and Terms pages. Content is frozen from here.
- **Live site confirmed:** the client confirms in the group (SOP step 7).

## Extra work (quoted before it is done)

- Revision rounds beyond two, or changes after **Approved**.
- Custom pages and homepage sections (e.g. Equipment). Not extra: services or
  projects under the client's own name (Practice Areas, Case Studies) — that
  is wording.
- Anything that needs a new component or layout: new section types,
  alternative layouts, custom animations.
- Translating, sourcing or editing photos, creating a logo.
- Multiple languages, a blog or admin panel, online payments, an embedded
  booking calendar (a booking link is included), a cookie consent banner.
- Email accounts.
- Changes after the first year beyond the agreed yearly plan
  ([`handoff.md`](handoff.md)).
