## Process for this client: surprise website (decided 2026-11-18)

This is not the normal process. The client has not asked for anything and there is no kickoff call.

1. Branding, light: research the sector, then decide primary color, palette and fonts only. No full Brand Guideline document yet.
2. Project Blueprint, as the second working material, built from research and the client's existing website.
3. Website from the Aksumite template, made presentable with what we have.
4. Biniam deploys it on Vercel (no real domain) and sends the client the link as a surprise.
5. Only if the client likes it and wants to continue: ask for real information (real testimonials, proof, logo files), then run the normal process (full Brand Guideline etc.).
   Until then: no kickoff questions; use only public and existing material; never present unverified facts (such as the website's testimonials) as real.

## Kickoff questions

(15 minutes, with Enqubahir Kassaye Teferra)

1. Year the firm started, and your own qualifications (degree, ACCA/CPA, AABE licence type, years, former employers such as the tax office?).
2. Do you sign statutory audits yourself, or prepare clients for another auditor?
3. How many clients now, what kinds (sector, size), and can 2 or 3 be named?
4. How do most clients find you, and why do they say they stayed?
5. What tax-assessment results can you share (anonymous is fine), e.g. assessments reduced or disputes settled?
6. Team size, and who does the work?
7. Do you want to keep the current logo and blue/green colors?
8. Which email and phone are official? Any social media pages?
