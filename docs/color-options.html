Enqubahir Kassaye: main color options

Each option uses the same hero, the same text (taken from his current website) and the same fonts, so only the colors change. The contrast figures are calculated live from the hex codes on this page. The hero headline is a draft for comparing colors, not final copy.

1. How the main color is chosen

Test Why it matters for this firm Evidence
1. He recognises it as his This is a surprise website. If he opens the link and it does not feel like his business, he will not continue. His existing material is navy, plus a green tick in the logo and red on the flyer. Logo navy about #03284E; flyer navy + red (research.md)
2. It does not look like a rival A client comparing firms in Addis should not confuse him with Parity, HLB or Dessalegn. Peer colors measured from their live sites (below)
3. It says “safe with the tax office” His buyers fear penalties and assessments. The color must read calm, exact and serious. Red is ruled out as a main color: in accounts it means loss and errors. Bright or playful colors are ruled out. Services: VAT, WHT, PAYE, assessment representation (profile)
4. It works on screens White text on the main color needs at least 4.5 contrast. The accent must stand out on the main color and on dark mode (at least 3). Most visitors will be on phones. Template minimums (check-colors.mjs)
